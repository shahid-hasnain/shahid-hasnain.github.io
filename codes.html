Magneto-nanolubricant peristaltic flow solver

Companion code for Hasnain, Al-Atawi & Saqib, Scientific Reports (2026)

A finite-volume solver for peristaltic flow of synovial fluid between the wavy membrane and the cartilage of the knee: velocity, temperature, nanoparticle concentration, induced magnetic field and a FitzHugh–Nagumo-type neural-activation field, with three non-Newtonian viscosity models. Every governing equation and boundary condition is written out in the code, next to the step that solves it.

  • Beavers–Joseph slip or no-slip walls, convective (Biot) heat exchange, and a positivity-preserving scheme for the concentration
  • dp/dx from the flux constraint, and the free-pumping flow rate (zero net pressure rise over one wavelength), both by secant iteration
  • Includes bolus_results.py, which draws the Bolus Results figure shown here
  • The Bolus Results figure is an illustrative demonstration, not a figure from the article

Python · NumPy · Matplotlib · MIT licence

Gold-nanoparticle synovial flow solver

Companion code for Alshammari, Hasnain & Saqib, AIMS Mathematics 11(7) (2026) 22543–22575

A finite-volume solver for flow, heat, nanoparticle concentration and induced magnetic field in a wavy, porous annular channel of the knee joint, with a temperature- and concentration-dependent Carreau–Yasuda viscosity and second-order slip at the outer wavy wall. Every step in the code quotes the article equation and boundary condition it implements, so you can follow the mathematics line by line.

  • Solves Eqs. (3.7)–(3.17) of the article; the pressure gradient is found by direct superposition at a fixed flow rate, exact at convergence
  • A corrected version of the code used for the article: the radial grid follows the wavy wall, and the source terms follow the signs of Eqs. (3.9)–(3.10), so numbers differ slightly from the published figures
  • Includes temperature_results.py, which draws the Temperature Results contour shown here
  • The Temperature Results figure is an illustrative demonstration, not a figure from the article

Python · NumPy · SciPy · Matplotlib · MIT licence
